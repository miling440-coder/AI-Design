## Video direction

- Palette: warm-stone paper `bg-primary` throughout; ink `text-primary` for all people and decisive words; taupe `line`/`accent` for pressure, paths, rings and secondary labels; `bg-secondary` only for the house silhouette and option zones.
- Type: Playfair display for the one dominant thought in each frame; Inter body/label for small social voices, dates and option labels. Stick figures use the same ink + taupe hairline language.
- Motion grammar: smooth long-tail arrivals (`power3` feeling), explicit `fromTo` states, and voice-paced progressive reveal. Each new person, bubble, path or word appears only when spoken; nothing is dumped into the first quarter.
- Rhythm: Frames 1–3 accumulate pressure, Frame 4 deliberately breathes and holds after the clarification, Frame 5 expands into possibilities, Frame 6 becomes sparse and still for the final question. Holds are static except for bounded subtle jitter on the active stick figure.
- Caption keep-out: every meaningful element remains in the top ~83% of the 4:3 canvas; captions own the bottom band.
- Never: colored gradients, rounded SaaS cards, drop shadows, bouncing entrances, infinite loops, lazy breathing, late camera drift, front-loaded slideshow timing, or independently floating screensaver motion.
